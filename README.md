# PrepLoop

PrepLoop is an app for practicing UPSC Civil Services Examination PYQs.

## Available papers

The bundled paper archive is organised by stage, paper, and year. The year shown is the UPSC examination year.

| Stage | Paper | Available years |
|---|---|---|
| Prelims | General Studies Paper I | 2011–2026 |
| Prelims | CSAT | 2013–2026 |
| Mains | Essay | 2011–2025 |
| Mains | General Studies I | 2013–2025 |
| Mains | General Studies II | 2013–2025 |
| Mains | General Studies III | 2013–2025 |
| Mains | General Studies IV | 2013–2025 |
| Mains | Mathematics Optional — Paper I | 2011–2025 |
| Mains | Mathematics Optional — Paper II | 2011–2025 |

Mathematics is currently the only optional subject available. Other optional subjects are not included.

Prelims include timed tests and practice mode. Mains papers are available for viewing.
